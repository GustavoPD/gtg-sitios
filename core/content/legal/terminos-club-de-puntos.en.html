Benefits. Use of the same

3.1. The benefits of the Club are as follows:

a) Exclusive offers and promotions: Club members will receive offers and promotions to the e-mail address indicated at the time of registration. Only the Club member will be able to make effective use of these promotions. The offers, discounts and promotions will not be cumulative. Promotions or offers may consist of special discounts or other types of benefits.

b) Points Club: Club members may earn Points that will be redeemable for the Benefits described herein. The Club is subject to the following special conditions in addition to the other conditions of the Club:

Welcome Points: once the member is duly registered, 10 (ten) Points will be awarded.

Only nights paid at a qualifying rate and booked through the website, reservation center or the front desk of any hotel (hereinafter "Qualifying Stays") will generate Points.

Points are not awarded for stays booked through third party websites (such as, but not limited to, Booking, Expedia and Despegar) or for reward nights, bonus nights, bookings made by tour operators or stays based on sponsorship rates or other special arrangements such as "Group Booking". No other item invoiced by the Group will generate points.

Club members must provide their membership number to generate points.

If a hotel ceases to be a Participating Hotel after a Club member makes a reservation, the stay will not qualify for earning Points.

For the sole purpose of calculating the points to be accumulated, the club will take the amount invoiced in Argentine pesos for the stay (not including taxes, fees, commissions, extra charges) and convert it to dollars at the selling exchange rate published by the Banco de la Nación Argentina on the business day prior to the date of invoicing. For such amount calculated in U.S. dollars and already paid, the club member will obtain 15% of such value in points.. For example, if a member purchases a night for 100 USD, that member will earn 15% as points which will equal 30 points. Points are equivalent to 15 USD and can be used from the next reservation. Points will not be awarded for reservations paid with points. With partial payment, i.e. payment with points plus payment in currency, Points are awarded based on the portion of the purchase not paid with points.

Points will be deducted from the Club member's account at the time the redemption request is made.

All exchanges are final and cannot be returned for credit.

Discounts are not redeemable for cash or other benefits.

Validity of Points

The validity of the points will begin to count from the date on which they are credited to the member's account. The expiration date of the points will be exactly 24 months after the accrual date.

Members will be notified of the expiration of their points through emails sent to the address registered in their account and/or through notifications on the program's mobile application or website. These notifications will be sent early enough to allow members to use their points before they expire.
